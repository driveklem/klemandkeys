Introduction

Last Updated: December 27, 2024

Welcome to Klem & Keys Realty Limited ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

By accessing or using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our services.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
  • Identification Information: Government-issued ID, passport details (when required for property transactions)
  • Financial Information: Bank account details, payment information (processed securely through third-party payment processors)
  • Property Preferences: Budget, location preferences, property type interests
  • Communication Records: Correspondence with our team, inquiry history, feedback

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect:

  • IP address and device information
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website addresses
  • Operating system information

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide property listings, schedule viewings, process transactions, and deliver requested services
  • Communication: To respond to inquiries, send property updates, and provide customer support
  • Personalization: To tailor property recommendations based on your preferences and search history
  • Marketing: To send newsletters, promotional offers, and market insights (with your consent)
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with Nigerian laws, regulations, and legal obligations
  • Security: To protect against fraud, unauthorized access, and other security threats
  • Analytics: To improve our website, services, and user experience

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of sensitive data during transmission (SSL/TLS)
  • Secure storage systems with access controls
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Employee training on data protection practices
  • Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small data files stored on your device.

Types of Cookies We Use:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for website functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our website
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences
  • Marketing Cookies: Track your browsing to show relevant advertisements

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

Your Privacy Rights

Under Nigerian data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request access to your personal information we hold
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Objection: Object to processing of your personal information for marketing purposes
  • Data Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service provider
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.
