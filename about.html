Our Story

Building Dreams, Creating Value

Founded in 2015, Klem & Keys Realty Limited has established itself as a premier real estate company in Nigeria, specializing in property development, sales, investment advisory, and comprehensive real estate solutions.

Our journey began with a simple vision: to transform the real estate landscape by providing exceptional service, transparent transactions, and value-driven property investments. Today, we have successfully facilitated over 30 property transactions with a portfolio worth billions of Naira.

We combine deep market knowledge with innovative solutions to help our clients make informed decisions and achieve their real estate goals.

Our Leadership

Meet Our Founder

Adedokun Clement

Senior Property Consultant & Founder

Adedokun Clement is a passionate and ambitious real estate professional with over 10+ years of experience in the Nigerian property market. His journey began with a simple vision: to create a real estate company that prioritizes integrity, transparency, and client satisfaction above all else.

As a visionary leader, Clement combines deep market knowledge with innovative strategies to help clients make informed property investment decisions. His hands-on approach and commitment to excellence have been instrumental in building Klem & Keys Realty into one of Nigeria's most trusted real estate brands.

Under his leadership, the company has successfully facilitated hundreds of property transactions, helping countless families and investors achieve their real estate dreams while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism and ethical conduct.
